# Demo product photos

The product photos in this folder are used only by the "Load demo shop (dev)"
button. They are front-of-pack images from the crowd-sourced
[Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) and
[Open Beauty Facts](https://world.openbeautyfacts.org) databases, each retrieved
by its barcode (see `src/seed.ts`). The images are licensed
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) — credit goes to
the contributors who took them. Trademarks and product imagery belong to their
respective owners.

`logo.png` is a sample shop logo generated for the demo (an Ionicons storefront glyph).
