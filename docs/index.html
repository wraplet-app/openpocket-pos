Everything a counter needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built for mini marts, grocers, cafés, kiosks and home businesses — the shops that can't wait for a connection.

Fast billing

Tap or scan items into a cart. Cash, card, transfer or store credit, with change and quick-cash suggestions.

Real inventory

Photos, categories, SKUs, barcodes and per-product low-stock and out-of-stock alerts.

Barcode scanning

Scan to sell or to add a product. A built-in lookup fills the name and image, saved for offline use.

Staff & roles

Owner, manager and cashier PINs. Every sale is attributed to the cashier — on screen and on the receipt.

Receipts & invoices

Thermal 58/80mm or A4, themed to your shop. Print over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or USB, or share a PDF invoice.

Reports & profit

Daily, weekly and monthly sales, gross profit, refunds, discounts, payment mix and best sellers.

Multiple shops

Run several separate shops on one device — each with its own products, staff, receipts and books.

Cloud sync (optional)

Back up your shop and sync across devices for $2/month. Offline selling stays free.

Dark mode

Full light and dark theming across every screen, so the counter is easy on the eyes at any hour.
