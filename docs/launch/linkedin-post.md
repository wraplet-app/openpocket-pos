## Version A — the problem-first story (recommended)

Most point-of-sale apps ask a small shop to do three things before they can ring
up a single sale: create an account, get online, and start a subscription.

For a corner store with patchy Wi-Fi, that's three reasons to give up.

So I built **OpenPocket POS** — a point-of-sale that runs entirely on a phone,
offline, with no account required.

→ Sell, scan barcodes, take cash/card/credit, and print or share a receipt
→ Track inventory, staff and profit — the numbers a shop actually watches
→ Works with zero internet; every sale is stored on the device
→ Free forever for offline selling. Cloud backup and multi-device sync is $2/month
→ Open source (MIT), and the money math is exact — integer minor units, never floating point

It's built with React Native, TypeScript and SQLite, with an optional
Cloudflare Worker for sync.

Free, offline-first, and open to anyone who wants to build on it:
👉 https://wraplet-app.github.io/openpocket-pos/

If you run a shop, or know someone who does, I'd love your feedback.

## Version B — the punchy launch

Launching **OpenPocket POS** 🏪

A free, offline-first point-of-sale that lives in your pocket.

No account. No internet required. No monthly fee to sell.

Install it, name your shop, and start ringing up sales — cash, card, transfer or
